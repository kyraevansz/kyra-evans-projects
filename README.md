# Kyra Evans - Projects
A collection of my data science, research, and competition projects, including work in public health analytics, science policy, and AI-driven health tools.

## Projects

- **Ambient** — 1st Place, VSU Hackathon (Spring 2026). AI-powered Multiple Sclerosis health app with passive symptom tracking, automated caregiver alerts, and a provider dashboard.
- **EcoAI** — 3rd Place, AI Innovation Challenge (Mar 2026). Data-driven tool helping Northern Virginia residents understand data centers' impact on energy costs, with bill-reduction insights and a policy advocacy layer.
- **[Data Science for the Public Good](./data-science-public-good/shinydashboard.R)** — Analyzed public health datasets to identify community clusters and built an R Shiny dashboard for stakeholders; findings informed a policy memo on food deserts and health disparities presented to VASEM members and legislators (./data-science-public-good/shinydashboard.R).
- **The Yard Fund** — Full-stack PHP/MySQL web application connecting VSU students with tuition balances to donors and alumni for direct giving. Built with session-based authentication, role-based access control, Chart.js donor analytics, and automated email delivery via PHPMailer.

## Tech Stack

Python, R, Java, HTML
